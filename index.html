The Problem

Education Is Broken at Scale

India adds 10 lakh people to the workforce every month — yet 99% of what students study is disconnected from real aptitude and career readiness.

Traditional education, especially for competitive exams, prioritizes grades over understanding. Standardized curricula fail to address how learning actually works: knowledge emerges from experience, not passive memorization.

The brain forgets irrelevant information to conserve energy. Disconnected history, geography, and unused languages are wasted without tying to real problems or personal aptitude. The system produces mismatched skills at massive scale.
